# Cinematic still review checklist

## Read at thumbnail size

- Is the subject or visual event recognizable immediately?
- Is there one clear center of attention?
- Does the silhouette read without inspecting detail?
- Does negative space feel intentional?

## Inspect at full resolution

- Are faces, hands, text, reflections, and repeated structures coherent?
- Do contact points and object relationships make physical sense?
- Does the subject retain the required identity, materials, or product geometry?
- Is detail concentrated where the frame needs it rather than spread uniformly?

## Composition and depth

- Do foreground, subject plane, and background separate cleanly?
- Does the eye move through the frame in the intended order?
- Do shot size, perspective, and depth of field agree?
- Is the chosen aspect ratio helping the composition?

## Light and color

- Is the key source visible or inferable?
- Does light interact with a surface, atmosphere, or edge?
- Are highlights and shadows plausible for that source?
- Is the palette controlled, with accent colors earning attention?

## Editorial utility

- Does the frame communicate the intended moment without explanation?
- Is it distinct from a generic beauty image?
- Does it support the intended crop, text-safe area, or placement?
- Would it still work if the word “cinematic” were removed from the prompt?

## Verdicts

- **Select:** the moment, hierarchy, light, and crop all work.
- **Iterate:** the contract is sound, but one named variable needs correction.
- **Reroute:** identity, layout, or composition needs stronger reference control.
- **Reject:** the image is polished but does not communicate the intended frame.
